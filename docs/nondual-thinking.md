# Nondual thinking — source canon

Derivation for `NONDUAL.md` (@-included in global CLAUDE.md). Goal: the cognitive operations behind "nondual thinking" with the metaphysics stripped out. What survives secularization is one repeated move — differentiate the poles, then integrate them under stated conditions — plus discipline about frames. What got cut: consciousness-as-fundamental, all-is-one ontology, soteriology — out of scope for a thinking doctrine, not adjudicated.

## The dichotomy habit is cultural, and measurable

- Peng & Nisbett, *Culture, Dialectics, and Reasoning About Contradiction* (American Psychologist 54(9), 1999). Five experiments: European-American subjects polarize under contradiction (lay-Aristotelian — determine which side is correct); Chinese subjects seek a middle way. Both fail differently: polarization discards a true pole, naive compromise retains a false one. The doctrine keeps Western differentiation AND Eastern integration; each alone is a half-thinker.
- Nisbett, *The Geography of Thought* (2003). Book-length treatment: analytic-object vs holistic-field cognition.

## Making "nondual" operational

- Suedfeld & Tetlock — integrative complexity. The scoreable version: differentiation (how many distinct perspectives recognized) × integration (how they connect — trade-offs, synthesis, superordinate schema). Low IC = single fixed frame. High IC is not fence-sitting: integration requires prior differentiation.
- Grossmann — wise reasoning (*Wisdom in Context*, Perspectives on Psychological Science 2017; Grossmann, Gerlach & Denissen, SPPS 2016). Intellectual humility + perspectives beyond the issue + change/context sensitivity + integration of opinions. Predicts judgment quality independently of IQ; self-distancing raises it.
- Tetlock, *Expert Political Judgment* (2005). Foxes (many small models) out-forecast hedgehogs (one master frame). Master frames are dichotomy generators.

## The logic end — contradiction without mysticism

- Priest, *The Fifth Corner of Four* (OUP 2018); Priest & Garfield, *The Logic of the Catuṣkoṭi* (Comparative Philosophy 1(2), 2010). Nāgārjuna's tetralemma — true / false / both / neither — formalized in First Degree Entailment, a four-valued paraconsistent logic. The "A or B?" option space is provably ≥ 4; contradiction tolerance is a logic, not a mood.
- Bohr — complementarity. Two mutually exclusive descriptions, both necessary, neither complete. Physics' license for both/and.
- Korzybski, *Science and Sanity* (1933). The "two-valued orientation" critique; map/territory. General semantics grew its own cult — take the lens, leave the movement.

## Dialectic as developmental stage

- Riegel, *Dialectic Operations* (Human Development 16, 1973); Basseches, *Dialectical Thinking and Adult Development* (Ablex 1984). Dialectical thinking as the postformal successor to Piaget's formal operations: schemata for reasoning about motion through forms rather than choosing between fixed forms.
- Kegan, *The Evolving Self* (1982), *In Over Our Heads* (1994). The subject-object move: growth = converting the frame you think WITH (subject) into a frame you can inspect (object). Dualities are properties of the current subject.

## Applied deployments

- Johnson, *Polarity Management* (HRD Press 1992). Problems have solutions; polarities have management. Interdependent poles — each pole's upside requires the other — so choosing one degrades both. The polarity map is the working tool.
- Smith & Lewis, *Toward a Theory of Paradox: A Dynamic Equilibrium Model of Organizing* (Academy of Management Review 36, 2011 — Decade Award); *Both/And Thinking* (HBR Press 2022). Paradox = "contradictory yet interrelated elements that persist over time"; sustained both/and outperforms either/or resolution.
- Martin, *The Opposable Mind* (2007). Integrative thinking: hold two opposing models in tension until a third emerges containing elements of both, superior to each.
- Linehan, *Cognitive-Behavioral Treatment of Borderline Personality Disorder* (Guilford 1993) — DBT. The most operationalized secular dialectic: acceptance AND change as the core clinical synthesis, RCT-backed.
- Beck / Burns — cognitive distortions. Dichotomous (all-or-nothing) thinking is a canonical distortion, "splitting" its clinical extreme. Dualism as measurable pathology, not just style.

## The perceptual end (kept, quarantined)

- Dunne, *Toward an Understanding of Non-Dual Mindfulness* (Contemporary Buddhism 12(1), 2011); Josipovic, *Nondual awareness: consciousness-as-such as non-representational reflexivity* (Progress in Brain Research 2019). Contemplative science of nondual awareness proper — collapse of subject/object structuring in perception. Empirical study of the phenomenon the traditions name; its metaphysical claims were not imported.
